Evaluation Protocols

Two complementary protocols are used. The first matches the literature and makes results comparable with published numbers. The second removes the session leakage that the first allows and measures what a deployed sensor would face: drones and environments recorded in sessions the model has never seen.

1. Protocol 1 – Standardised 10-Fold Segment-Level Cross-Validation

Data
All 22,700 segments from the 227 synchronised receiver pairs
Splitting
StratifiedKFold(n_splits=10, shuffle=True, random_state=1) across segments
Per fold
20,430 training segments (90%) and 2,270 test segments (10%), class proportions preserved
Seed
1
Reported metrics
Mean ± standard deviation of accuracy, macro-F1 and (for VARDHAN-v3) balanced accuracy over 10 folds; confusion matrix pooled over all 22,700 test predictions
Models evaluated
FGCS2019DNN, MC1DCNN, VARDHAN-v3

Caveat. Segments are assigned to folds independently, so segments from the same recording appear in both training and test sets. This is the protocol used by the original papers, but it measures within-session interpolation rather than generalisation to new recordings.

2. Protocol 2 – Strict Recording-Level Benchmark (Zero Leakage)

Data
454 files from 23 distinct recording sessions
Splitting
Sessions assigned exclusively to one split: 15 training (308 files), 4 validation (73 files), 4 test (73 files)
Overlap
Recording overlap 0, file overlap 0
Seed
42
Model selection
Checkpoint with minimum validation loss
Split files
data/splits/train.csv, val.csv, test.csv (generated by src/data/create_splits.py)

The class composition of each split is given in Dataset §5. In this split the Phantom class appears on different receiver bands in training and test (Dataset §6).

5. Metrics

  • Accuracy – fraction of correctly classified segments.
  • Macro-F1 – unweighted mean of per-class F1; sensitive to minority-class (Phantom) performance.
  • Balanced accuracy – unweighted mean of per-class recall; equals 25% for a constant predictor on four classes.
  • Per-class precision, recall and F1, and the confusion matrix.

Implemented in src/evaluation/metrics.py (calculate_metrics, generate_confusion_matrix).
