Signal Preprocessing

Each model in the project consumes a different input representation. This page documents the common per-segment pipeline, the faithful re-implementation of the original FGCS 2019 feature extraction, and the model-specific representations. Implementations live in src/preprocessing/ and src/data/.

1. Segmentation

Each DroneRF CSV file contains 10,000,000 samples. For the standard benchmark, every file is divided into 100 non-overlapping blocks of 100,000 samples, giving 100 segments per synchronised (L, H) pair and 22,700 segments overall. Waveform-based models (DSCNN, VARDHAN) take the first 2048 samples of each 100,000-sample block (offset i × 100,000), i.e. a 20.48 µs window at 100 MS/s.

2. Generic Per-Segment Pipeline

The following operations are applied independently to every 2048-sample segment x[n], n = 0 … N−1, N = 2048.

Step 1 – DC mean detrending

xd[n] = x[n] − (1/N) Σn x[n]

Step 2 – Per-segment normalisation

xnorm[n] = xd[n] / √( (1/N) Σn xd[n]2 + 10−8 )

Step 3 – Real fast Fourier transform

X[k] = Σn xnorm[n] · exp(−j2πkn/N),   k = 0, 1, …, 1024

The rFFT yields 1025 complex bins from DC to Nyquist (50 MHz at Fs = 100 MS/s).

Step 4 – DC bin removal

The DC bin (k = 0) is dropped and the 1024 positive-frequency bins k = 1 … 1024 are retained.

3. Faithful FGCS 2019 Feature Pipeline

To reproduce Al-Sa’d et al. (2019) exactly, the original MATLAB scripts Main_1_Data_aggregation.m and Main_2_Data_labeling.m were re-implemented in src/preprocessing/fgcs_faithful.py (optionally JIT-accelerated with Numba for CSV parsing).

  1. Synchronised L/H pairing – pair the lower-band (2.40–2.44 GHz) and upper-band (2.44–2.48 GHz) files of each recording.
  2. 100,000-sample segmentation – split each 10,000,000-sample file into 100 non-overlapping segments.
  3. DC removal – subtract the segment mean from both the L and H segments.
  4. 2048-point FFT and shift – apply fftshift and keep the positive-frequency half (1024 bins per band).
  5. Q = 10 boundary matching – scale the H spectrum by c = mean(xf[end−9:end]) / mean(yf[1:10]) and concatenate [xf ; c·yf], giving 2048 bins spanning 2.40–2.48 GHz.
  6. Power conversion – square the stitched magnitude spectrum.
  7. Global normalisation – divide by the global maximum (per-BUI mode max scaling, as in Main_2_Data_labeling.m).
  8. Class ordering – 0 Background (BUI 00000), 1 Bebop (100xx), 2 AR (101xx), 3 Phantom (11000).

The MC1DCNN reproduction reuses the same spectrum and divides the 2048 bins into 8 uniform sub-bands of 10 MHz each, producing an (8, 256) input.

5. VARDHAN-v3 Three-Channel Spectral Representation

VARDHAN-v3 computes its spectral input on the fly inside the network (VardhanV3.extract_spectral_representation in src/models/vardhan_v3.py), so the same raw waveform tensor feeds both branches and no spectral features are cached. After mean detrending, a 2048-point rFFT is taken, the DC bin is dropped, and the 1024 remaining bins are mapped to three channels:

Table 2. Spectral channels (N = 2048, k = 1 … 1024).
ChannelDefinitionPurpose
0 – Log powerP[k] = ln(1 + 1000 · |X[k]|2 / N)Compressed dynamic range of the power spectrum
1 – Real partR[k] = Re(X[k]) / √NRetains phase information discarded by the power spectrum
2 – Imaginary partI[k] = Im(X[k]) / √NCompletes the complex Fourier coefficient

The resulting tensor has shape S ∈ ℝB×3×1024.

6. Leakage Controls

  • Any dataset-level normalisation statistics (μ, σ) are fitted only on the training split or training fold; validation and test data use the frozen training statistics.
  • Per-segment detrending and normalisation are computed independently for every segment and therefore cannot leak information between splits.
  • No preprocessed NPZ caches are shared across splits; DroneRFLazyDataset (src/data/loader.py) loads and transforms segments lazily on demand.
  • The strict protocol additionally isolates entire recording sessions (see Dataset §5).

Normalisation matters. For VARDHAN-v3, replacing per-segment normalisation with a single global train-fold Z-score collapsed accuracy from 71.57% to 45.55% and Background recall to 0%. Global statistics let high-amplitude transmitters (e.g. the Phantom) dominate the scaling. See Results §6.
